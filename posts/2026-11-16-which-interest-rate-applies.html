Don’t use today’s Bank Rate for late-invoice interest

If a business customer pays you late, you may be able to add statutory interest at 8% plus the official reference rate — not whichever Bank Rate happens to be in force today.

For interest that starts to run between 1 July and 31 December 2026, that reference rate is the Bank of England rate in force on 30 June: 3.75%. So the statutory rate is 11.75%. A later change in Bank Rate does not alter the figure for this half-year.

This only applies to business-to-business invoices, including public authorities, not to private individuals. Always put a clear due date on the invoice. This is general information, not legal advice.

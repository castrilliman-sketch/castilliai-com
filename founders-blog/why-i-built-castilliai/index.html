Why I built Castilliai

Daniel Wyatt · 18 November 2026

I’ve spent 20 years working in finance in London. That time has been in debt purchasing, complaints handling and credit control for a consumer loan company, and I’ve also trained and managed teams of new starters.

What that gave me was a view from both sides. I’ve seen how businesses and regulators talk, and I’ve seen how hard that language can be for the people on the receiving end. Technical wording that makes sense inside an office can leave someone unsure what they actually have to do.

The gap I kept seeing

Take Making Tax Digital for Income Tax. It is a big change for landlords and sole traders. Whether it applies to you, and when, depends on your qualifying income: your self-employment and property income added together, before expenses. It has applied since 6 April 2026 to people with qualifying income over £50,000 in the 2024 to 2025 tax year. From 6 April 2027 it applies if that income was over £30,000 in 2025 to 2026, and from 6 April 2028 if it was over £20,000 in 2026 to 2027. Once you are in, you keep digital records and send quarterly updates.

Those dates are on GOV.UK. What is harder to find is a clear way to choose software. HMRC publishes a list of recognised products and does not recommend any of them. A lot of what is written about that choice is either jargon, or a sales pitch for one product.

Small regulated firms face the same problem from the other side. From 1 January 2027, firms start collecting data for the FCA’s new complaints return. Large firms have compliance teams for that. Many small consumer credit and debt firms don’t.

What Castilliai does

I built Castilliai to close that gap. We turn financial rules into plain English. Our first two products follow from that: MTD Software Compare, a free comparison of 179 HMRC-recognised Making Tax Digital for Income Tax products for landlords and sole traders, and the Consumer Credit & Debt Complaints and Arrears Toolkit for small FCA-regulated firms.

I use AI to gather and organise the details quickly, but a person always checks the results against the original source before anything goes on this site. Where we may earn a commission, we’ll always say so, and it never decides how products are presented.

Thanks for reading. If there’s a rule you’d like explained in plain English, email me.

Daniel

Dates and thresholds checked against GOV.UK and FCA publications as of 18 November 2026.

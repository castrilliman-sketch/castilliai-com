The key facts

11.75%

Statutory interest

Reference rate 3.75% (Jul–Dec 2026), statutory rate 11.75%. The current Bank Rate (4% since 5 Nov 2026) applies from 1 Jan 2027 if it is still in force on 31 Dec.

£40 · £70 · £100

Fixed sum per invoice

£40 if the debt is under £1,000, £70 for £1,000 to £9,999.99, £100 for £10,000 or more — once per invoice, on top of interest.

B2B only

Business customers

These rights apply when your customer is a business or public authority, not a private individual. If no payment date was agreed, a payment is late after 30 days.

Sources: Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998, GOV.UK late commercial payments guidance and the Bank of England. Reference rate 3.75% (Jul–Dec 2026), statutory rate 11.75%.

Questions

Who is Get Paid UK for?

UK sole traders, freelancers and small suppliers who invoice other businesses or public authorities and want a clear, calm way to chase late payment themselves. It is not written for consumer debts or for anyone who wants a third party to contact their customers.

Can I add interest to an invoice for a private customer?

Not under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998. Statutory interest and the £40 / £70 / £100 fixed sums only apply when both you and your customer are acting in the course of a business, including public authorities.

What is the statutory interest rate at the moment?

Reference rate 3.75% (Jul–Dec 2026), statutory rate 11.75%. The current Bank Rate (4% since 5 Nov 2026) applies from 1 Jan 2027 if it is still in force on 31 Dec.

Questions

Who is Get Paid UK for?

UK freelancers, contractors and small businesses who invoice other businesses or public authorities and want a clear, calm way to chase late payment themselves. It is not written for consumer debts or for anyone who wants a third party to contact their customers.

Can I add interest to an invoice for a private customer?

Not under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998. Statutory interest and the £40 / £70 / £100 fixed sums only apply when both you and your customer are acting in the course of a business, including public authorities.

Questions

Who is this for?

UK freelancers, contractors, sole traders and small businesses owed money by another business or a public authority. It is not for consumer debts.

Can I really add interest to a late invoice?

Usually, yes. Under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998 you can claim statutory interest at 8% above the Bank of England reference rate (11.75% for debts that became late between 1 July and 31 December 2026), plus fixed compensation of £40, £70 or £100 per invoice, unless your contract sets a different rate. It does not apply to consumer customers.

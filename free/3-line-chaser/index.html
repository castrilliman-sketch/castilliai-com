The 3-line chaser for late invoices

A short note for the day after a business invoice falls due. Fill in the blanks and send it yourself.

Day 1 — the day after the due date

Send these three lines:

  1. A polite reminder that invoice [number], for £[amount], was due on [date].
  2. Please pay it today, or tell me when it will be paid.
  3. You can pay by bank transfer. The bank details are on the invoice.

If it is still unpaid

  • Day 7. A firmer reminder. Repeat the invoice number, the amount and the due date, and ask again for payment or a date.
  • Day 14. A final reminder. Mention that under UK late payment law you can claim statutory interest and fixed compensation. Interest is 8% plus the reference rate (11.75% for July to December 2026).
  • Day 30. A formal letter before action, or the next step, such as a small claim via GOV.UK Money Claim Online.

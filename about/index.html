About

Daniel Wyatt.

Founder of Castilliai.

I’ve spent 20 years working in finance in London, in debt purchasing, complaints handling and credit control for a consumer loan company, and I’ve also trained and managed teams of new starters. That means I’ve seen money matters from both sides: how businesses and regulators talk, and how hard that language can be for the people on the receiving end.

I started Castilliai to close that gap. Rules on tax, credit and complaints affect landlords, sole traders and small firms every day, and most of what’s written about them is either jargon or a sales pitch. Castilliai turns that into plain English and lays out the options, so you can decide what suits you.

I use AI to gather and organise the details quickly, but a person always checks the results against official sources before anything is published. Where we may earn a commission, we’ll always say so, and it never decides how options are presented.

What we’re building

  • MTD Software Compare: a free, plain-English comparison of HMRC-recognised Making Tax Digital software for landlords and sole traders. Launching soon.
  • Consumer Credit & Debt Complaints and Arrears Toolkit: templates and tools for small FCA-regulated consumer credit and debt firms. From £79. Waitlist open.

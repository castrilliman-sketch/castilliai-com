Why now.

New return, new data.

The FCA is replacing its complaints returns with a single return (COM001). Data collection starts on 1 January 2027, so the log in use from that date matters.

The format may still change.

On 13 November 2026 the FCA withdrew its COM001 technical guides while it reviews the format. We say so plainly, and toolkit buyers get a free update once it is final.

Arrears in focus.

How firms treat customers in financial difficulty is a regulatory focus. Clear forbearance records help show what was done and why.

Questions.

Who is it for?

Smaller FCA-regulated firms without an in-house compliance team, around 22,200 of which the FCA expects to report through COM001.

Is this “COM001-ready” or FCA approved?

No, and we won’t claim either. The FCA does not approve products like this, and it withdrew its COM001 technical guides on 13 November 2026 while the format is reviewed. The log is built to help collect the data the new return asks for from 1 January 2027, and toolkit buyers will get a free update once the FCA finalises the technical format.

Which rules does it relate to?

Complaints handling under the FCA’s Dispute Resolution: Complaints sourcebook (DISP), and arrears, default and recovery under CONC 7. Always check the latest FCA Handbook and guidance at fca.org.uk.

Why now

New return, new data

The FCA is replacing its complaints returns with a single return (COM001). Data collection starts on 1 January 2027, so the log you use from that date matters.

The format may still change

On 13 November 2026 the FCA withdrew its COM001 technical guides while it reviews the format. We say so plainly, and toolkit buyers get a free update once it is final.

Arrears under the spotlight

How firms treat customers in financial difficulty is a regulatory focus. Clear forbearance records help you show what you did and why.

Questions

Is this “COM001-ready” or FCA approved?

No, and we won’t claim either. The FCA does not approve products like this, and it withdrew its COM001 technical guides on 13 November 2026 while the format is reviewed. The log is built to help you collect the data the new return asks for from 1 January 2027, and toolkit buyers will get a free update once the FCA finalises the technical format.

Is this legal or compliance advice?

No. These are templates and tools you adapt and use yourself. They are not legal, regulatory or compliance advice, and they don’t replace your own judgement or professional advice. Your firm remains responsible for meeting the FCA rules that apply to it.

Which rules does it relate to?

Complaints handling under the FCA’s Dispute Resolution: Complaints sourcebook (DISP), and arrears, default and recovery under CONC 7. Always check the latest FCA Handbook and guidance at fca.org.uk.

When will it be available, and what will it cost?

It isn’t on sale yet. We expect the full toolkit to be about £199 and the Letters Pack about £79, one-off. Waitlist members will hear first and get an early-access price.

Is this connected to Get Paid Faster?

Both are made by NexusAI t/a CastilliAI. Get Paid Faster is for UK businesses chasing late B2B invoices. This toolkit is a separate product for FCA-regulated firms.

Questions

Who is this for?

UK freelancers, contractors and small businesses who invoice other businesses or public authorities and want a clear way to chase late payment. It is not written for consumer debts, debt-collection agencies, or for anyone who wants a third party to contact their customers.

Does statutory interest apply to consumer customers?

No. The interest rate and fixed recovery fees under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998 apply to business-to-business (and business-to-public-authority) transactions. They do not apply where your customer is a consumer. The templates and calculator flag that clearly. If your contract already sets a different late-payment interest rate, statutory interest may not apply, except that you cannot agree a lower rate with a public authority.

Where do the interest figures come from?

From the 1998 Act and from GOV.UK guidance on late commercial payments. Statutory interest is 8 percentage points above the Bank of England reference rate: the base rate in force on 30 June for debts that become late from 1 July to 31 December, or on 31 December for debts that become late from 1 January to 30 June.

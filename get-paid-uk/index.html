Questions

Who is this for?

UK sole traders, freelancers and small suppliers who invoice other businesses or public authorities and want a clear way to chase late payment. It is not written for consumer debts, debt-collection agencies, or for anyone who wants a third party to contact their customers.

Does statutory interest apply to consumer customers?

No. The interest rate and fixed recovery fees under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998 apply to business-to-business (and business-to-public-authority) transactions. They do not apply where your customer is a consumer. The templates and calculator flag that clearly. If your contract already sets a different late-payment interest rate, statutory interest may not apply, except that you cannot agree a lower rate with a public authority.

Is this legal advice? Do you contact my customers?

No, and no. Get Paid UK is general information and a set of tools for you to use yourself. It is not legal advice and it is not a substitute for a solicitor or other qualified professional. Where do the interest figures come from?

From the 1998 Act and from GOV.UK guidance on late commercial payments. Statutory interest is 8 percentage points above official Bank of England Bank Rate. The Bank Rate used in the calculator is 4% from 5 November 2026, following the Monetary Policy Committee meeting ending 4 November 2026 — see bankofengland.co.uk. By default the calculator applies the six-monthly statutory reference rate (the rate in force on the previous 30 June or 31 December). Always check for a later Bank Rate decision before you invoice for interest.
